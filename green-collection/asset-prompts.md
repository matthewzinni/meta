# Scene-prop generation prompts

Built-in image-generation tool used. The book photographs were never generated or redrawn. These prompts create illustrative scene props only. Every atlas was visually inspected and separated into individual props for controlled compositing. The food props are atmosphere, not verified book recipes.

## Shared atlas specification

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a single square transparent PNG prop atlas for premium tactile paper-cut stop-motion book advertisements. Four completely isolated objects in a strict 2 by 2 grid with large transparent gutters. Each fully fits inside its own quadrant, no overlap. Photorealistic tabletop studio lighting from upper left; dimensional handcrafted paper and real object materials; elegant art direction, beautiful material texture, subtle shadows, clean silhouettes. No books, words, letters, numbers, logos, backdrop, sheet or border. Genuine transparent alpha background.

## Nutrition atlas, final revision

Create a production-ready square transparent prop atlas for stop-motion ads, four small isolated objects at the centers of four quadrants. Critical: each object must fit inside just the central 60% of its quadrant; leave an enormous empty transparent cross-shaped gutter between objects, and at least 10% canvas margin. Upper left a single bowl of vegetables and grains. Upper right two lemons with mint leaves. Lower left a single tall clear glass of pink citrus mocktail. Lower right a single dark skillet of vegetables. Photoreal studio tabletop product photography, beautiful materials, no background, no words, no logos, no books. Absolutely no object, stem, handle, leaf or shadow may cross into any other quadrant. Transparent alpha.
