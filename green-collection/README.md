# Layitflat green collection

26 books selected from actual green effective cell fills in the supplied spreadsheet, read October 23, 2026. Five versions per book: 130 concepts, 390 full-resolution MP4s, and matching JPG thumbnails and timed storyboards.

Open index.html in a browser. Published review: https://matthewzinni.github.io/meta/green-collection/. Choose a book and format, play previews, download MP4s/JPGs, and mark review selections. Selections persist in that browser; Export selections writes the actual chosen copy, format, filenames, product link, CTA and review status. Keep the entire folder together so the gallery links work.

## Production

12 seconds, H.264, 24fps delivery with deliberate 8fps stop-motion poses. 1080×1920, 1080×1350, and 1080×1080. Closing composition holds unchanged from 9.75s to the end. Sound-off legibility plus original synthesized tactile effects; no third-party music used.

Source cover photos match each spreadsheet ISBN and Shopify variant. Original cover RGB is preserved in internal cutouts; only exterior alpha changes. Export resampling and video compression naturally change decoded display pixels. White cover printing and spiral binding are protected. Only source-facing front views and in-plane rotation are used, without invented unseen sides or interiors.

New scene props were generated with the built-in image-generation tool; five existing subject prop sets from the prior campaign were reused as the motion reference. All background designs, typography and motion are composited deterministically. Food/household/study props are illustrative atmosphere, not proof of specific book contents or results. Current source-resolution images are 1500×1500 or 2000×2000.

## Review flags

Some authentic covers contain badges, counts or wording that differ from the product title and description. We preserve the cover and avoid added count claims. Unicorn Mermaid's cover says FOR ADULTS although the listing title says for girls; its added copy is age-neutral. Health-related books use product/educational framing without treatment or outcome claims. Fact-ledger.json lists per-title issues. Current platform/account eligibility and placement previews remain unverified. No ads have been launched.

creative-plan.md lists the exact selected titles and buying motivations. copy-table.csv contains the proposed copy. production-prompts.md includes every individual brief. campaign.json is the complete machine-readable manifest. quality-report.json records completed technical checks and the limits of visual review.

asset-prompts.md records the scene-prop generation briefs. The final ZIP contains materialized files, not iCloud placeholder links.

## Web distribution

Videos are web encodes using H.264 CRF 20, keeping 1080-pixel width, original aspect ratios, 24fps, audio, and duration. The original full campaign ZIP and source-asset working files are kept outside this repository. Downloadable JPGs are the original full-resolution JPEG bytes. Earlier reviews and their assets remain unchanged.
